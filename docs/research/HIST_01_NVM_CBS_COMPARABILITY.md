# HIST-01 — NVM / CBS comparability

**Decision: no approved splice.** NVM-linked historical series and CBS/Kadaster nationwide notarised transactions are not interchangeable merely because both report a house price.

## Evidence and definitional risks
- Vastgoedmarkt's historical annual table labels **average sale price of existing homes**, 'per ultimo', in thousands of euros. Verify whether 'per ultimo' means a year-end estimate rather than a transaction-weighted annual mean; clarify retrospective EUR conversion, housing mix, and sampling.
- ESB publishes an NVM Wonen **median** series (1985–1999 selected years) in guilders, a distinct statistic. Median cannot be substituted for average.
- CBS 85773NED / 83906NED distinguish average sales price from price index, based on national Kadaster transactions and WOZ for the index. CBS explicitly cautions that the average sales price is not an indicator of constant-quality house-price evolution.
- A CBS methodology report describes NVM coverage before 2000 at roughly 50% of the market; historical coverage and composition require verification, and that estimate must not be blindly extrapolated to 1975. Source: https://www.cbs.nl/-/media/imported/onze-diensten/methoden/dataverzameling/aanvullende-onderzoeksbeschrijvingen/documents/2015/49/2015-groningenveld-methoderapport.pdf
- CBS annual 1995 price is EUR 93,750 (85773NED), whereas Vastgoedmarkt table 1995 is EUR 105,000 (thousands-of-euros table). **Descriptive difference**: EUR 11,250, ~12% relative to CBS. This is a cross-publication diagnostic only, not a validated like-for-like matched-source statistic. Different population, timing and retrospective presentation remain unresolved.
- The separately validated 1995–1999 CBS monthly segment may be used for a future comparison, not automatic normalization or joining.

## Required controlled overlap study
Obtain original NVM metadata and observations; document average/median, type, national coverage, transaction vs asking, timing, annual aggregation, vintage and licensing. For every 1995–1999 overlap year compare only same statistic and same temporal window, report differences, ratio, variation, and structural breaks with raw input hashes. Test house-mix sensitivity. No synthetic backcast.

## Options
1. **Preferred conditional research:** distinct annual historical NVM/Vastgoedmarkt segment with conspicuous source and frequency break, alongside independent monthly CBS segment.
2. Linked series only after source provenance, comparable population and independently justified linkage accepted in a separate decision.
3. Annual-only historical view, clearly labeled, if metadata/rights permit.
4. No historical extension if original source and FX cannot be verified.

No data changed.
