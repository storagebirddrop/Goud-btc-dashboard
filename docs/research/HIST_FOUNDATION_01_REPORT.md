## 2. Executive decision report

**Ready for owner decision (method, not publication):**
1. Gold before 2000 in guilders: construction C1 (World Bank USD gold x Fed monthly-mean NLG/USD, 1975-01..1998-12, quality levels A/B) with C4 from 1999-01; C3 only as a labelled nominal euro equivalent. The empirical error bound against the ECB-based series (median 0.058%, max 0.307% over 1999-2001) is small relative to gold's own volatility. ROOD (source priority, FX, aggregation).
2. Bitcoin monthly method: arithmetic mean of daily closes (not month-end: differs mean 7.8-9.5%), EUR-native Bitstamp from 2016-05 (Q1), USD-derived V1 with ECB from 2013-04 (Q2), earlier months Q3 research-only; quality levels and tolerances (about 2% typical, 5% hard fail from 2014). Gaps stay null; no automatic splice.
3. Housing: keep CBS average price (levels, gold/BTC per house) and CBS HPI (relative performance) as separate concepts; no combined NVM+CBS series; any NVM material only as separately labelled annual segments.
4. Equity: concept A (relative performance rebased at the earliest common observation), total return in EUR vs CBS HPI, monthly from 2000-01; concept B (index points per house) is not presentable. S&P 500 and AEX have different credible starts; a joint chart starts at the later one.

**Needs more evidence before any decision:** NVM level series 1975+ (blocked, written permission and a text table needed); direct guilder gold prices (C2); gold lineage before 1979; pre-1970 national house prices (none exist); free AEX total return and S&P net total return.

**Open owner decisions that truly need approval:** (D1) rights path for each third-party source (World Bank GOLD is accepted risk since GOLD-03; Bitstamp, Kraken, Yahoo, Euronext, S&P DJI, NVM are not), because derived values reconstruct the source price; (D2) approve or reject construction C1 and the 2.20371 redenomination for NLG-era values; (D3) Bitcoin fixing definition (daily close, UTC) and start month per quality level; (D4) whether an index-only 1970-1994 view is acceptable; (D5) where layer-1 research files may live and whether release authorization is a JSON file or a DECISIONS entry; (D6) CPI series for real values (ADR-009); (D7) whether to request NVM written permission and S&P/Euronext licences.

## 3. Architecture (ARCH-02)

`etl/hist_model.py` (stdlib only, 739 lines) implements four layers: source observations, normalized series, derived series and a publication layer whose allowlist is **empty**; `convert_frequency` always raises (no annual-to-monthly), there is no fill or interpolation function, redenomination is applied exactly once and recorded, derived comparisons require a common baseline from the actual overlap, price-return/total-return and nominal/real/average-price/index kinds cannot be mixed, revisions are detected not overwritten, hashes are canonical sha256. `tools/hist_model_check.py` validates a research bundle (exit 1 on violation). New assets are added as adapters that emit `hist-model/1` bundles; no per-asset publication pipeline. The track tools (`hist_06`, `hist_07`, `btc_02`, `eq_01`) are research probes with their own pure functions; their outputs have **not yet** been converted to ARCH-02 bundles (next milestone).
